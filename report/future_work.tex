\subsection*{Possible extensions}
A small follow-up could vary one implementation choice at a time:
commander reverse implications, the product cutoff, or a valid
symmetry-breaking constraint. Any symmetry restriction should be applied
consistently to all compared models, and its effect on finding one
placement should be distinguished from enumerating all placements.
A longer deadline would also show whether the present timeout boundary
persists beyond the short-budget setting. Finally, completion instances
with fixed queens, including satisfiable and infeasible boards, would
provide a different test family. These are proposed extensions; none is
part of the measurements reported here.
